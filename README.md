## 🧩 Featured Projects

<b>Laprovia — SaaS for Field Operations and Technical Evidence Management</b>

A private SaaS product focused on field operations, technical activities, evidence management, material control, approvals, audit logs, and standardized reports.

The project explores real-world product architecture, multi-tenant concepts, mobile-first workflows, role-based access control, operational dashboards, and field execution processes.

Main areas: SaaS, Backend, React, APIs, SQL, Mobile-first, Reports, Audit Trail, Operations Management.

###
<b>Navalia — BarberShop's Scheduling and Business Management Platform<b/>

A SaaS project for barber shops focused on appointment scheduling, customer management, professional schedules, services, availability rules, and business operations.

The roadmap includes AI-powered features such as customer support automation, internal assistant actions, schedule insights, and operational commands through natural language.

Main areas: SaaS, Scheduling, Multi-user System, Backend, React, AI Integration, Business Automation.
